The Story

From backyard smoker to Comstock Road

Crosstown started as a backyard smoker and launched as an East York pop-up in April 2025 — drops sold out in hours. On November 7, 2026, pitmaster and owner Rueben Silva opened the first permanent Crosstown Craft BBQ at 51 Comstock Road in Scarborough, with an upgraded 750-gallon smoker and the same sell-out energy.

  • ✓

    Texas basics, done properly — brisket and ribs are the stars, smoked low and slow over white oak.

  • ✓

    A different feature every day — Fridays bring a feature dish, Saturdays are for beef dino ribs, Sundays for smoked and seared picanha.

  • ✓

    Open until sold out — the posted closing time is the latest the counter stays open; the BBQ decides.
